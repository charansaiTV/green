Introduction

A smart green campus turns sustainability into everyday action. Students and staff can work together to save energy, reduce waste, conserve water, and care for the environment.


Green Campus Activities

  • Plant trees and care for campus gardens.
  • Organize campus clean-up days.
  • Use reusable bottles, lunch boxes, and bags.
  • Join recycling and environmental awareness events.

Solar Energy

Solar panels convert sunlight into electricity. Using solar power on campus can reduce reliance on fossil fuels and help lower pollution.


Waste Management

Good waste management keeps the campus clean and reduces the amount of rubbish sent to landfills.

  • Separate paper, plastic, glass, and food waste.
  • Recycle materials whenever possible.
  • Compost fruit and vegetable scraps.
  • Avoid single-use plastics.

Water Conservation

Everyone can help save water by turning off taps, reporting leaks, and using only the water they need. Collecting rainwater for gardens is another helpful idea.


Environmental Tips

  1. Switch off lights and fans when leaving a room.
  2. Walk, cycle, or carpool when possible.
  3. Bring reusable items instead of disposable ones.
  4. Keep the campus green and put rubbish in the correct bins.
